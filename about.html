Hi, I'm travis, a macro & wildlife photographer based in Seattle, Washington.

In the quiet world of small creatures, a complex and often unnoticed beauty thrives. My work seeks to reveal this delicate and intricate life, magnifying the vibrant ecosystems of our world. I invite viewers to step into the microscope and witness the whimsical proboscis of a butterfly, the iridescent sheen of a dragonfly, or the solemn gaze of a crow.

In a time when biodiversity is dwindling at an alarming rate, I aim to create more than just portraits. These images are meant to provoke reflection; a meditative pause in our chaotic world where the beauty of life is slipping away unnoticed. My photographs act as elegies for what we stand to lose if we do not act.
